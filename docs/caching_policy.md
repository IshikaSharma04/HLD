# Caching Strategy

- **Pattern:** Cache-Aside with Write-Through strategy for hot read data.
- **Eviction Policy:** `allkeys-lru` (Least Recently Used).
- **Cache Hit Target:** > 95%
- **TTL:** 3600s with random jitter to prevent cache stampede.
